NeoInbox

Google Inbox is back.

Bundles, trips, pins, snoozes and reminders: Inbox by Gmail, rebuilt as a Thunderbird add-on that works with any email account.

Try the live demo View on GitHub

That's the real app, running on a sample mailbox right in your browser. Go ahead: open a bundle, mark something done, pin a message. Open it full screen

The live demo runs the real app on a sample mailbox, and it's made for a bigger screen. Try it on a computer, or open it here anyway.

Bundles

Receipts, statements, newsletters and notifications gather into bundles you can clear in one click. Make your own bundles, and teach them by moving mail.

Trips

Flight, hotel, train and dinner confirmations come together as one trip, whether it's next week or last year.

Done, pin, snooze

Mark mail done to clear it. Pin what matters. Snooze the rest until tomorrow, the weekend, or whenever you need it.

Reminders

Jot down to-dos right in your inbox, next to the mail they're about.

Package tracking

Shipping emails get a card with a tracking link. UPS, USPS, FedEx, Yamato and more are recognised automatically.

Private by design

Sorting happens on your computer. No servers, no telemetry, no account to sign up for. Your mail stays in Thunderbird.

Get NeoInbox

NeoInbox needs Thunderbird 115 or newer and works with any account Thunderbird can open: Gmail, Outlook, Fastmail, or your own server.
